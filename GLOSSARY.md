# No Heroes Below

A kobold community defends its home against invading adventurers through preparation, positioning, and traps.

## Language

**Lair**:
The kobold community's home and the place it defends against adventurers.
_Avoid_: Mission hub, expedition base

**Defense**:
An encounter in which the kobolds prepare their lair and resist an invading adventuring party.
_Avoid_: Field mission, expedition

**Preparation**:
The opportunity before a raid to position the crew and prepare the cave's defenses.

**Kobold crew**:
The individual kobolds commanded together to defend their community.
_Avoid_: Adventuring party, heroes

**Adventurers**:
The invaders entering the kobolds' lair.
_Avoid_: Allied heroes

**Pantry**:
The lair's food supply room, which the kobolds may need to protect during a raid.

**Whistle**:
Piks the Scout's ability to draw a visible adventurer toward a marked tile.

**Quick Rig**:
Mumpf the Trapper's ability to prepare a trap during a defense.

**Stone Shot**:
Krix the Slinger's ranged attack that pushes an adventurer on a hit.
