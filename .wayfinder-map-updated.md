## Destination

A playable Godot proof of concept of **Waking in the Warren**, the first encounter in the No Heroes Below pitch: prepare a trap, command kobolds against invading adventurers, understand d20 attacks and saving throws, see who is attacking whom and which actions can reach which targets, and reach a restartable win or loss.
